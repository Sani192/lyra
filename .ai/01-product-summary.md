# 01 Product Summary

## What Lyra Is

Lyra is a voice AI orchestration platform that receives conversations, identifies intent, selects declared capabilities, invokes consumer systems, and records observable conversation evidence.

## What Lyra Is Not

Lyra is not a CRM, ERP, ordering system, appointment system, payment processor, inventory system, or consumer-specific workflow engine. It does not own business state or encode consumer business policy.

## Business Goals

* Reduce the risk of deploying AI voice workflows in production.
* Make integrations repeatable through contracts.
* Give operators visibility into decisions, invocations, failures, and handoffs.
* Allow many domains to integrate without forking Lyra into bespoke applications.

## Target Users

Platform engineers, integration engineers, AI product teams, operations teams, compliance reviewers, and consumer application owners.

## Long-Term Vision

Lyra becomes the trusted orchestration layer for customer conversations across protocols, domains, and AI providers while preserving clear ownership boundaries.

## MVP

The MVP is documentation-first: stable requirements, domain language, contract conventions, capability registry design, conversation lifecycle, observability requirements, and reference examples.

## Future Roadmap

Future work includes contract validation tooling, capability registry implementation, protocol adapters for REST/OpenAPI/MCP, runtime orchestration, dashboards, event streams, SDKs, and deployment automation.
