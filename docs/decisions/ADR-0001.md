# ADR-0001: Why Lyra Is Stateless

## Maturity Metadata

**Status:** Approved.

**Intended Use:** Authoritative architecture decision; safe for implementation decisions within its documented scope.

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Lyra must orchestrate conversations without becoming a source of truth for consumer domains. Lyra must remain a general orchestration platform across organizations, projects, environments, and domains.

## Problem

Without this decision, future contributors could add hidden assumptions, consumer-specific behavior, or database tables inside Lyra that hold customer details, sales inventory, or financial ledgers. This creates synchronization bottlenecks, data redundancy, and security compliance issues.

## Options Considered

1. **Stateful Database within Lyra:** Store copies of consumer business entities (e.g. customer accounts, tables list, item availability) in a local database within Lyra to enable fast lookups and offline capability. Rejected because it leads to data synchronization drift, compliance bloat (e.g., holding PCI/HIPAA data), and violates the stateless boundary.
2. **Pure Ephemeral Orchestration (No Persistence):** Lyra operates purely in-memory. Once a call turn completes, Lyra retains no records, transcripts, or session metadata, delegating all memory to the client. Rejected because it blocks multi-turn slot filling, webhook callbacks, and analytics tracking.
3. **Stateless with Metadata Persistence:** Lyra stores conversation session metadata, turn logs, transcripts, and audio recordings, but never stores authoritative business state. Accepted because it maintains orchestration capability without absorbing consumer business records.

## Decision

Store only orchestration metadata, transcripts, recordings, analytics, events, contracts, and invocation evidence; never store authoritative consumer business state.

## Consequences

* **Simplified Security Posture:** Lyra does not require complex database encryptions or security scopes for consumer transactions (such as credit cards or health histories).
* **Horizontal Scalability:** Core runtime services are stateless and can scale horizontally without database synchronization overhead.
* **Separation of Concerns:** Clear boundary between conversation orchestration (Lyra) and business domain logic (consumer application).
* **Sync Elimination:** Lyra never synchronizes database tables with consumer ERPs or CRMs.

## Future Considerations

Verify this stateless architecture through test assertions verifying that no database schemas or models inside Lyra represent business-of-record entities. Ensure all data adapters strictly manage conversation transcripts and audio bytes only.
