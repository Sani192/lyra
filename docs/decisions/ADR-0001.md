# ADR-0001: Why Lyra is Stateless

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Lyra must orchestrate voice conversations across many consumer applications without becoming a bespoke implementation for each one.

## Decision

Lyra stores orchestration metadata, transcripts, recordings, analytics, and events, but does not store authoritative consumer business state.

## Consequences

* Platform boundaries remain clear for engineers and AI agents.
* Consumer applications retain ownership of their domain correctness.
* Lyra can evolve orchestration capabilities independently of consumer business rules.
* Contracts, observability, and compatibility become mandatory design concerns.
