# ADR-0001: Why Lyra Is Stateless

## Status

Accepted.

## Context

Lyra must orchestrate conversations without becoming a source of truth for consumer domains. Lyra must remain a general orchestration platform across organizations, projects, environments, and domains.

## Problem

Without this decision, future contributors could add hidden assumptions, consumer-specific behavior, or protocol coupling that makes Lyra harder to operate, audit, and extend.

## Options Considered

1. Embed domain behavior in Lyra for speed. Rejected because it would make Lyra a bespoke application and create ownership ambiguity.
2. Delegate all orchestration to consumer applications. Rejected because it would remove Lyra's platform value and observability model.
3. Keep Lyra focused on contract-driven orchestration with explicit ownership boundaries. Accepted because it preserves reuse, safety, and traceability.

## Decision

Store only orchestration metadata, transcripts, recordings, analytics, events, contracts, and invocation evidence; never store authoritative consumer business state.

## Consequences

* Platform boundaries remain clear for humans and AI agents.
* Consumer applications retain ownership of domain correctness.
* Lyra can evolve orchestration capabilities independently of consumer business rules.
* Contracts, observability, validation, and compatibility become mandatory design concerns.

## Future Considerations

Future implementation must prove this decision through schema validation, contract tests, observability events, compatibility checks, and examples that demonstrate safe failure handling.
