# 21. Observability

## Maturity Metadata

**Status:** Draft.

**Intended Use:** Design specifications for logging and metrics.

## Purpose

Define the logging formats, trace spans, metrics gathering, and observability adapters.

## Scope

Covers system logs and metrics emission.

## Business Motivation

Allows operators to monitor platform health and conversation drop-offs in real-time.

## Assumptions

- Traces conform to OpenTelemetry standards.
